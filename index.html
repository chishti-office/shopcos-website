Best Shop Software in Pakistan — Point of Sale (POS) Billing Software for Every Business

Looking for shop software in Pakistan? shopcos soft is the complete point of sale (POS) software designed for Pakistani retailers. Whether you run a kiryana store, medical store/pharmacy, garment shop, or any retail business — our billing software handles everything from fast barcode billing to khata/udhar management.

Point of Sale Software That Actually Works Offline

Unlike cloud-based POS software that stops working without internet, shopcos soft runs 100% on your shop computer. Your shop billing software keeps working during load-shedding, internet outages, and slow connections. Every bill, every stock update, every khata entry — saved locally, always accessible.

Medical Store Software with Batch & Expiry Tracking

Running a pharmacy? Our medical store software tracks batch numbers, generic names, and expiry dates automatically. Get alerts before medicines expire, manage supplier accounts, and never lose money on dead stock again. The best pharmacy software in Pakistan for independent medical stores.

Shop Khata Software — Udhar Management Made Simple

Stop losing money on unrecovered credit. Our shop khata software tracks every customer's udhar balance, payment history, and credit limits. See who owes what at a glance, get alerts for risky debtors, and recover every rupee with professional khata management.

Kiryana Store Software with Smart Stock Control

Kiryana store software needs to handle thousands of small items. shopcos soft manages stock levels, purchase rates, expiry dates, and low-stock alerts automatically. The AI predicts stock-outs before they happen, so you never lose a sale.
