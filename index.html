Best Shop Software — Point of Sale (POS) Billing Software for Every Business Worldwide

Looking for shop software? shopcos soft is the complete point of sale (POS) software designed for retailers in 31 countries. Whether you run a grocery store, pharmacy, garment shop, mobile shop, electronics store, or any retail business — our billing software handles everything from fast barcode billing to customer credit management. Available in 28 currencies with multi-language support.

Point of Sale Software That Actually Works Offline

Unlike cloud-based POS software that stops working without internet, shopcos soft runs 100% on your shop computer. Your shop billing software keeps working during power outages, internet disruptions, and slow connections. Every bill, every stock update, every credit entry — saved locally, always accessible. When internet returns, everything syncs to the cloud automatically.

Pharmacy Software with Batch & Expiry Tracking

Running a pharmacy or medical store? Our pharmacy software tracks batch numbers, generic names, and expiry dates automatically. Get alerts before medicines expire, manage supplier accounts, and never lose money on dead stock again. The best medical store software for independent pharmacies — from Pakistan to Nigeria to the Philippines.

Credit Ledger Software — Customer Account Management Made Simple

Stop losing money on unrecovered credit. Our shop credit management tracks every customer's balance, payment history, and credit limits. See who owes what at a glance, get AI-powered alerts for risky debtors, and recover every penny with professional ledger management. Called "khata/udhar" in South Asia — the concept works everywhere.

Grocery Store Software with Smart Stock Control

Retail shop software needs to handle thousands of items. shopcos soft manages stock levels, purchase rates, expiry dates, and low-stock alerts automatically. The AI predicts stock-outs before they happen, so you never lose a sale — whether you sell rice in Karachi, medicine in Lagos, or electronics in Manila.
