Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. Introduction

shopcos soft ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it — for both our website and our software.

2. Website Data

When you sign up on our website, we collect your email address and store it securely in our database (Supabase). We use it to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Send verification codes (OTP)
  • Send your license key
  • Provide customer support

We never sell your email or share it with third parties for marketing.

3. Software Data

Your shop data stays on your computer. The shopcos soft software runs 100% offline on your own PC. Your products, sales, customers, and financial data never leave your computer unless you choose to back up to your own Google Drive.

4. License Validation

When you enter a license key, the software contacts our server once to verify it. Only the license key is transmitted — no shop data.

5. Cookies

Our website uses browser local storage to keep you logged in, and cookies for analytics and advertising as described below. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time; the website will still work, though some features may be limited.

6. Advertising

We show advertisements on some pages of this website (such as the homepage and blog) through advertising partners, including Google AdSense and Monetag.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Our advertising partners may collect non-personal information such as your browser type, device, and approximate location in order to measure and improve their ads.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

7. Google Sign-In

If you sign up with Google, we receive your email address and name from Google. We do not access any other Google data.

8. Your Rights

You can request deletion of your account and data at any time by emailing chishtioffice2@gmail.com.
