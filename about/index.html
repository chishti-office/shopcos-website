About shopcos soft

shopcos soft is smart shop management software built for small retailers across Pakistan. We help kiryana stores, pharmacies, cloth shops, and every kind of retail business run professionally — with fast billing, stock tracking, khata/udhar management, and AI-powered insights.

Our Story

shopcos soft was created by COS — Chishti Office Samundri, a technology company based in Samundri, Punjab, Pakistan. We saw small shopkeepers struggling with paper registers, lost udhar records, and expired stock — while expensive software was built for big chains, not for the common dukandaar.

So we built software designed for them: simple enough for any staff member to learn in a day, powerful enough to transform how a shop runs, and priced as a one-time payment — not a monthly burden.
